## What I learned

- Search-term specificity matters more than filters for LinkedIn discovery — a generic job title ("financial analyst") is a weak signal on its own since it exists in every industry; a niche/specific title ("equity research analyst") is a much stronger filter by itself
- Not every "match" is real — one "financial analyst" result was actually a recruiter whose profile summary happened to contain matching keywords, not an actual analyst
- Seniority is a tradeoff in cold outreach: VP/Director/MD-level contacts give the most valuable perspective if they respond, but individual-contributor analysts are statistically more likely to have 15 free minutes for a stranger's call
- Personal/warm contacts (2 friends added to the list) are easier wins but don't build or validate the cold-outreach skill Day 7 is actually training — worth keeping them a minority of the list, not the majority
