---

## What I learned

- Chroma embeds text for you automatically; Pinecone doesn't — you generate the
  vectors yourself (with whatever model you choose) and upsert them alongside
  metadata. Pinecone is "bring your own embeddings," Chroma is batteries-included.
- Both tools cleanly separated all 10 topics on real queries — e.g. "Did the
  company have any employee layoffs?" pulled back layoffs-topic sentences only,
  and "Did the company return money to shareholders?" pulled back buybacks-topic
  sentences only, in both tools.
- Pinecone's results were slightly cleaner than Chroma's on the "legal risk"
  query (fewer cross-topic hits) — likely because `text-embedding-3-small` is a
  stronger model than Chroma's default local MiniLM model.
